FoldCraft fold-conditioning baseline

Independent reproduction of the 6-fold vs PD-L1 benchmark

Run 2026-06-15 · 6 folds × 100 designs (600 total) · single RTX 4090, ~9.5 h · prepared by Novel Therapeutics

This is a frozen, self-consistent config — not a bit-faithful reproduction of the paper. FoldCraft.py consumes binder/target_hotspots as 1-based chain positions, but Supp. Tables 1–2 give what look like PDB residue numbers (e.g. Ig-like/3SD2 "91-99" exceeds the 77-residue trimmed binder). We therefore trimmed each template to its chain-A range, renumbered to 1..N, and mapped the Supp.-table hotspots to positions; PD-L1 target hotspots are the Supp. Table 2 values applied to the shipped pd_l1.pdb. Absolute numbers are not claimed to match the paper — but they land squarely in the Fig. 3C regime (~0–27%), which validates the harness. We'd like to reconcile against your exact configs.

Method

  • Per fold: design_stages 100,100,20, 10 backbones × 10 ProteinMPNN sequences = 100 designs.
  • Target: PD-L1 (framework/test/pd_l1.pdb), target_hotspots = 34-39,43-49,11-17.
  • Evaluation: AF2-ptm (models 1 & 2, 3 recycles). Metrics pLDDT, ipTM, iPAE (normalized 0–1) from the design pipeline; RMSD = designed binder (chain B) Cα vs the fold template, superposed.
  • Success = pLDDT > 0.8 ∧ ipTM > 0.5 ∧ iPAE < 0.35 ∧ RMSD < 3.5 Å. CIs are Wilson 95%.

Key finding: fold fidelity is not the bottleneck — the interface is. Most folds reach RMSD<3.5 Å at high rates (barrel 92%, top7 86%, iglike 83%), so fold-conditioning is working; combined success is gated almost everywhere by interface confidence (iPAE is the tightest criterion). The large binders fare worst at the interface (tim 180 aa: iPAE<.35 only 1/100; ankyrin is the exception — weak on both fold and interface).
